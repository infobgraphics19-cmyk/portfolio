Our Hot Ones journey

From a Maui Backyard to the Wings of Doom

Adoboloco started with a garden, not a business plan. Tim and Summer Parsons were homeschooling their kids in Kīhei, Maui, and the class project was growing chili peppers. There were soon more peppers than the family could eat, so Tim turned them into the old-style Hawaiian chili pepper water he grew up on. The name came from his other childhood love: his mom's chicken adobo.

The first bottle was a simple Jalapeño sauce made with four ingredients: apple cider vinegar, peppers, sea salt and garlic. Friends asked for more. Then restaurants. Then grocery stores. That same less-is-more approach is still how we make every sauce today, in small batches, with peppers from local farms.

In 2018, Hamajang, our kiawe-smoked ghost pepper sauce, earned a spot on Hot Ones Season 5. Sean Evans and his guests came back for more. Kolohe Kid followed in Season 8, then Fiya! Fiya! in the Season 10 All-Star lineup. We teamed up with HEATONIST, the show's official hot sauce partner, to create Island Wings for Season 18. And in Season 21 we hit our Black Jack year with Jalapeño Chico.

Five seasons later, we're still the same family business, still bottling in small batches, and still amazed every time an Adoboloco wing hits the table.

Hamajang Kiawe Smoked Ghost Pepper Hot Sauce

The one that started our Hot Ones story. Hamajang is Hawaiian pidgin for "messed up," and that's how a ghost pepper makes you feel. We smoke the ghost peppers over kiawe wood, then keep the recipe simple: apple cider vinegar, peppers, sea salt and garlic. You get a deep, smoky heat with a bright, tangy finish.

Guests who took on Hamajang in Season 5 included Charlize Theron, Michael B. Jordan, John Mayer, Shawn Mendes, Tyra Banks and Alton Brown.

Try it on: loco moco, eggs Benedict, burgers, fried rice.

Kolohe Kid! Hot Sauce

Kolohe (co-low-hey) means "mischievous" in Hawaiian, and this sauce lives up to the name. Kolohe Kid blends ghost peppers with Hawaiian chili peppers for a thin, splashy, vinegar-forward sauce. Think of it as a spicier, more flavorful take on Tabasco.

Try it on: poke, seafood, tacos, Bloody Marys.

Fiya! Fiya! Hot Sauce

When Hot Ones invited its favorite makers back for the Season 10 All-Star lineup, we brought our hottest sauce yet. Fiya! Fiya! combines Trinidad Moruga Scorpion, smoked ghost peppers, habanero and sweet red jalapeños with fire-roasted garlic. The label says it best: Open. Go Fo Broke.

Try it on: spicy poke mayo, pizza, nachos, breakfast burritos, steak.

Island Wings Hot Sauce

Made with HEATONIST for Season 18, Island Wings is a taste of Hawaiʻi in one wing. Fire-roasted pineapple and honey meet chili peppers and red wine vinegar for a sweet, tangy, tropical sauce. It's the friendliest heat in our Hot Ones lineup and a perfect starting point for new fans.

Try it on: chicken wings, grilled shrimp, pork, summer barbecues.

Jalapeño Chico Hot Sauce

Season 21 was our Black Jack year, and Jalapeño Chico was our winning hand. Smoked jalapeños, smoked ghost pepper and Trinidad Moruga Scorpion bring the fire. A hint of lime, garlic and smoked paprika bring the flavor. It's a smoky, zesty taco sauce with serious heat.

Try it on: tacos, eggs, avocado toast, breakfast sandwiches, nachos.

Hot Ones FAQs

Which Adoboloco sauces have been on Hot Ones?

Five sauces across five seasons: Hamajang (Season 5), Kolohe Kid! (Season 8), Fiya! Fiya! (Season 10), Island Wings (Season 18) and Jalapeño Chico (Season 21).

What is the hottest Adoboloco sauce from Hot Ones?

By Scoville rating, Jalapeño Chico is highest at 103,000 SHU, followed by Fiya! Fiya! at 77,000 SHU. Fiya! Fiya! carries our top heat rating of 11/10 for how hard its scorpion pepper hits.

What is the mildest Adoboloco Hot Ones sauce?

Island Wings, at 5,500 SHU. It sat in the #2 spot in Season 18 and has a sweet pineapple flavor.

Where is Adoboloco made?

Adoboloco is a family-owned company founded in Kīhei, Maui, Hawaiʻi in 2011. We still make every sauce in small batches.

Yes. Shop them all on this page, or pick any three in our Build Your Own Trio box.
