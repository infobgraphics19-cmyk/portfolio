Nori Goma Furikake FAQs

What is Nori Goma Furikake?

A Japanese-style dry seasoning of toasted sesame seeds (goma) and nori seaweed, made island-style with a sweet, salty, umami balance.

How is it different from Nori Komi Furikake?

Nori Goma puts toasted black and white sesame first, for a nuttier, crunchier bite. Nori Komi is our classic, nori-forward rice seasoning.

Does it contain MSG?

No. It is also preservative-free, non-GMO and plant-based.

Is Nori Goma Furikake vegan?

Yes, it is plant-based with no animal ingredients.

Is it spicy?

No. It is savory, slightly sweet and salty, with no chili heat.

What can I put furikake on?

Rice, musubi, poke, sushi, ramen, eggs, fish, popcorn, salads and roasted vegetables.

Does furikake go bad?

It stays crisp longest sealed in a cool, dry place. Use it by the best-by date on the bag.
